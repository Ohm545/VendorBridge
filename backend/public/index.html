FAQ

Everything you need to know.

VendorBridge is a cloud-based procurement and vendor management ERP designed for mid-size to enterprise organizations. It's built for procurement officers, finance teams, operations managers, and vendors who need a unified platform to streamline purchasing workflows, vendor relationships, and financial reconciliation.

Procurement officers create structured RFQs with specifications, deadlines, and evaluation criteria. Assigned vendors receive a notification and submit their quotations through the vendor portal. VendorBridge then compiles all responses into a side-by-side comparison matrix — helping your team evaluate bids objectively and quickly.

Absolutely. VendorBridge includes a no-code workflow builder where admins can define multi-level approval chains, set budget thresholds for different approval tiers, assign approvers by role or individual, and configure auto-escalation rules when approvals are delayed beyond a set time window.

Yes. VendorBridge offers native integrations with popular accounting and ERP systems including Tally, SAP, QuickBooks, and Zoho Books. We also provide a REST API for custom integrations with your internal systems, HRMS platforms, or enterprise data warehouses.

VendorBridge has four default roles — Admin, Procurement Officer, Manager, and Vendor — each with scoped permissions. Admins can create custom roles, restrict module access per role, and configure field-level visibility. Every user only sees what they need, keeping sensitive procurement data secure.

VendorBridge uses AES-256 encryption for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit, and hosts on ISO 27001-certified infrastructure. We maintain SOC 2 Type II compliance, conduct regular penetration testing, and support SSO via SAML 2.0 for enterprise customers.

Our AI engine analyzes each vendor's bid across configurable criteria — price, delivery time, quality score, compliance status, and past performance — and applies weighted scoring to surface the best-fit recommendation. It explains its reasoning transparently, so procurement teams make informed decisions rather than just following a black-box output.

Vendors receive a dedicated portal link when assigned to an RFQ. They can submit quotations, track order status, and manage invoices without needing a full platform account. For recurring vendors, full onboarding unlocks a persistent vendor dashboard with communication history, document storage, and performance analytics.
